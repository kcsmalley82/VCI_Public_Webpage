For veterans & military families

VA home loans

VA loans are guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and are one of the strongest benefits earned through service.

  • A down payment may not be required for eligible borrowers with full entitlement
  • No monthly mortgage insurance
  • A one-time VA funding fee can be financed into the loan; veterans receiving VA disability compensation are generally exempt
  • Available to eligible veterans, active-duty service members, Guard and Reserve members, and some surviving spouses

Buy a primary residence using your VA benefit.

Flexible qualifying

FHA home loans

FHA loans are insured by the Federal Housing Administration and are built for buyers who want a lower down payment or more flexible credit guidelines.

  • Low down payment options
  • Flexible credit history and debt-to-income guidelines
  • Gift funds can help with the down payment and closing costs
  • Includes upfront and annual mortgage insurance premiums (MIP)

FHA Purchase

Buy a primary residence with a low down payment.

FHA Streamline

Lower the rate on your current FHA loan with reduced documentation.

FHA Rate-and-Term

Change your rate or term on an FHA loan.

FHA Cash-Out

Access equity in your primary residence.

Most popular

Conventional loans

Conventional loans follow Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines and work well for borrowers with solid credit, whether buying a first home, a second home or an investment property.

  • Low down payment options for qualified buyers, including first-time buyers
  • Private mortgage insurance (PMI) only when you put less than 20% down, and it can be removed as you build equity
  • Primary residences, second homes and investment properties
  • Fixed-rate and adjustable-rate terms

Cash-out refinance

Borrow against the equity you have built for home improvements, debt consolidation, education or other goals, with one monthly payment.

  • Available on VA, FHA and Conventional loans
  • Consolidate higher-interest balances into your mortgage
  • Your loan officer will show the total cost over time, not just the monthly payment
