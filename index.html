Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the program. VA loans may not require a down payment for eligible borrowers, and FHA and Conventional loans offer low down payment options. Try the payment estimator at the top of this page, and your loan officer will confirm what you qualify for.

What is the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?

A pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on what you tell us. A pre-approval means we have reviewed your credit, income and assets, which carries more weight with sellers and real estate agents.

Will applying affect my credit score?

A pre-approval includes a credit check. Mortgage inquiries made within a short shopping window are generally treated as a single inquiry by the credit scoring models. Ask your loan officer about timing.

Who is eligible for a VA loan?

Veterans, active-duty service members, certain members of the National Guard and Reserves, and some surviving spouses may be eligible. We can help you request your Certificate of Eligibility.

When does refinancing make sense?

A refinance can make sense when it lowers your payment enough to recover the closing costs in a reasonable time, shortens your term, removes mortgage insurance, or lets you use equity for a goal. Your loan officer can run the break-even math with you.

What documents will I need?

Usually recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank statements and a photo ID. See the checklist below.

Be ready

What you will need

Having these on hand speeds things up. Your loan officer will tell you exactly which apply to you.

Income

  • Pay stubs for the last 30 days
  • W-2s for the last two years
  • Tax returns if self-employed or paid on commission

Assets

  • Two months of bank statements, all pages
  • Retirement or investment statements
  • Gift letter, if family is helping

Identity & property

  • Driver's license or photo ID
  • VA Certificate of Eligibility or DD-214 (VA loans)
  • Current mortgage statement and homeowners insurance (refinance)
